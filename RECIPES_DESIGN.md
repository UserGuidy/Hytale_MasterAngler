# Master Angler - Crafting Recipes Design

This document outlines the recipes for creating the various Rod Components using raw materials.

## Rod Bodies (Corps)
*   **Oak Rod Body** (Tier 1)
    *   3x Oak Plank
    *   1x Leather (Handle)
*   **Fiberglass Body** (Tier 2)
    *   2x Fiberglass Sheet
    *   1x Iron Ingot
    *   1x Leather
*   **Carbon Fiber Body** (Tier 3)
    *   2x Carbon Fiber Mesh
    *   1x Titanium Ingot
    *   1x Leather

## Rod Lines (Lignes)
*   **Cotton Line** (Tier 1)
    *   3x String
*   **Braided Line** (Tier 2)
    *   2x Synthetic Polymer
    *   1x String
*   **Fluorocarbon Line** (Tier 3)
    *   3x Synthetic Polymer (Refined)

## Rod Reels (Moulinets)
*   **Basic Wooden Reel** (Tier 1)
    *   2x Oak Plank
    *   1x Iron Ingot
*   **High Speed Reel** (Tier 2)
    *   2x Iron Ingot
    *   1x Ceramic Parts
*   **Titanium Reel** (Tier 3)
    *   2x Titanium Ingot
    *   2x Ceramic Parts
    *   1x Carbon Fiber Mesh
